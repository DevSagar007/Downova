## 4. Decisions prepared (both **proposed**)

- **BRW-10:** [proposals/brw-10-private-capture-scope.md](../decisions/proposals/brw-10-private-capture-scope.md).
  - **Option A:** keep private capture unsupported in B2, with reworded acceptance (recommended for the B2 exit).
  - **Option B:** build an end-to-end ephemeral private mode.
  - Both list the effects on extension storage (records up to 7 days), service history, idempotency records
    (30 days) and recovery after restart.
  - BRW-10 stays `in-progress` until the maintainer chooses.
  - E02 needs no decision: its own priority line places Opera, Brave and Vivaldi QA in B4.
- **CORE-06:** [ADR 0024](../decisions/0024-bandwidth-limits.md), Proposed. It covers:
  - bytes/s as `ByteCount`; `null` means unlimited or inherit; bounds from 1,024 B/s to 10^12 B/s;
  - one global token bucket and one bucket per download shared by its segments; a 250 ms burst bound; chunks larger
    than the bucket charged in parts;
  - cancellation-safe waits that never count against `read_timeout`;
  - live changes through `watch`, with no restarts;
  - interactions with permits, rotation, pause/resume and restart;
  - the settings key plus migration `0010`, persist-then-apply;
  - capability gating (HTTP becomes `supported` only with the throughput tests; aria2 stays `unverified`);
  - contract, CLI and bilingual UI;
  - deterministic clock tests and ±10 % steady-state acceptance for 1, 3 and 6 downloads and 4 segments.
  - **Not started:** B3 needs B2 to exit first.

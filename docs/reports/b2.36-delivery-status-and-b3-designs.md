## 3. Designs finalized for B3.1 (both Proposed)

- **[ADR 0024](../decisions/0024-bandwidth-limits.md), bandwidth limits.** Reviewed; two defects in the design fixed:
  - **Burst floor:**
    - the first draft's capacity `max(rate × 250 ms, 16 KiB)` allowed a **16 s** burst at the 1,024 B/s minimum,
      contradicting its own quarter-second claim;
    - now `max(rate × 250 ms, 1 KiB)`: exactly 250 ms from 4,096 B/s up, never more than 1 s;
    - each part is taken from the per-download bucket and then the global one, with no pre-reservation.
  - **Throttled rotation:**
    - rotation is byte-based (`ROTATION_BYTES` = 1 MiB, `crates/backend-http/src/segmented.rs`), so at 2 KB/s a
      request would hold a host permit for about 512 s while another file waits;
    - now a validated range segment with waiters yields after **1 MiB or 2 s**, whichever comes first;
    - non-range streams still never rotate;
    - a test for it is specified.
- **[ADR 0025](../decisions/0025-queue-schedules.md), queue schedules (new).** It covers:
  - one schedule per queue, with an explicit IANA zone, overnight windows and merged overlaps;
  - pure evaluation in `dm-domain`, with the zone injected;
  - DST rules: a gap moves forward; in an overlap, start takes the earlier occurrence and end the later one;
  - `schedule_window` as a policy blocker only: never resumes a pause, never overrides a stopped queue, never revives
    a cancelled job, never bypasses limits;
  - resumable work is held at a window's end and non-resumable work finishes;
  - a 60 s bounded timer plus wake and clock-jump re-evaluation;
  - missed windows: `skip`, or one durable, bounded catch-up (the most recent missed window, within 24 h, at most
    6 h), recorded before it takes effect so a restart cannot repeat it;
  - persist-then-apply, and restore before recovery;
  - `jiff` proposed as a new direct dependency (licence review under REL-03);
  - contract, CLI and bilingual UI, including the off/asleep sentence;
  - a full verification plan.
